Cell Archetypes

Join by selecting a lineage that matches your biomineralization plan. Each archetype spawns with a unique silhouette and trait mix:

  • Mesenchymal Capsule - versatile starter that pivots quickly between cues.
  • Osteoblast Prism - mineral factory that converts nutrients into hydroxyapatite.
  • Osteocyte Dendrite - mechanosensitive networker with diagonal control bonuses.
  • Osteoclast Apex - high-motility resorber that trades stability for capture power.

Vitamin pellets (C, D, K, steroid) reshape motility and mineral gain in real time, while local cues (growth, nutrient, mineral, mechanical, hormonal, flow) steer differentiation.

Experimental Objectives

The HUD highlights culture-wide mineralization, the leading archetype, and active vitamin effects so lab partners can synchronize supply chains and keep the scaffold maturing.

Biomineralization Model Inputs

Bench conditions feed the simulation loop, anchoring each day of the MC3T3-E1 differentiation timeline with measured cues.

Base supplements push extracellular matrix assembly: Ascorbic Acid (Asc) drives collagen I synthesis and secretion to scaffold mineral growth, while β-Glycerophosphate (β-Gly) supplies phosphate donors that alkaline phosphatase converts into inorganic phosphate for hydroxyapatite precursors.

Boosting supplements fine-tune osteogenic commitment: Dexamethasone (Dex) accelerates differentiation when dosed carefully to avoid stress, and Vitamin K2 (MK-4) enhances mineral quality by promoting osteogenic gene expression and matrix organization.

Readouts calibrate the automata: ALP activity marks early commitment and phosphate liberation, collagen I immunofluorescence resolves ECM architecture at single-cell resolution, and Alizarin Red S staining quantifies late-stage calcium deposition. Together they provide thresholds and rate modifiers for the agent-based ruleset.

These inputs translate laboratory data into in-game parameters, letting players explore how supplements, enzyme kinetics, and matrix maturation couple to scaffold mineralization outcomes.
